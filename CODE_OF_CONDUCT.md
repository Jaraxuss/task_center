# Code of Conduct

We want TaskCenter to be a welcoming, useful project for people building context-aware task systems.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be open to correction.
- Keep discussions focused on the work and its impact.
- Prefer clear examples, reproducible reports, and practical tradeoffs.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing secrets, customer data, or private logs in public issues
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from project spaces.
